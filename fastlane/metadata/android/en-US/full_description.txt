Infra Arcana is a roguelike set in the early 20th century, inspired by the horror fiction of H.P. Lovecraft. Beneath a shunned and decrepit old church lie the sprawling catacombs of the Cult of Starry Wisdom. Descend to rob them of treasures and knowledge, and seek the artifact of non-human origin known as the Shining Trapezohedron - a window to all the secrets of the universe. Every expedition is procedurally generated, with scarce resources, permanent death, and horrors that erode your investigator's sanity.

This is the Android port with native touch controls - swipe to move, tap to confirm, drag to aim - a configurable action bar, and mobile-first menus and inventory.

The port is in early alpha: perfectly playable, but expect bugs or unrefinement.
